# Soccer ASCII artwork

The report uses eight section-bound SVGs made entirely of text characters. They scroll with their sections; the opening banner has no background artwork.

Photographs are sampled into 100 character columns, with row count adjusted for character-cell proportions. Luminance maps to ` .,:;+=xX%#@`. No generated artwork is used. The original photographs, authors, license URLs, and attribution are recorded in `soccer-legends.json` and displayed under the article's photo credits. ASCII adaptations retain each photograph's license.
